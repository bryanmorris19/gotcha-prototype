How to Play

Step 1 of 4

Follow today's clue

Search the store for the everyday product described by the clue. Every correct find begins a new hunt.

Step 2 of 4

Use Gotcha! Gold wisely

Earn Gold as you play. Spend it on an easier clue or another guess when a hunt gets tricky.

Step 3 of 4

Scan to verify your find

Tap the glowing scan button and line up the product barcode. Correct scans earn rewards and refresh your guesses.

Step 4 of 4

Complete the daily hunt

Correctly scan all three daily items to recover a stone fragment. Assemble the completed tablet to unlock the final scanner and recover a vault key.
