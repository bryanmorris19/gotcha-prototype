# Gotcha! Visual Style: Resonance Atelier

## Style Name

Resonance Atelier

## One-Line Definition

A clean painterly 3D adventure style for Gotcha!: warm cinematic Archive realism, expressive stylized characters, simplified hand-painted surfaces, brass-and-emerald resonance lighting, and mobile-readable shapes.

## Core Style Prompt

Use case: stylized concept art / in-game cinematic frame  
Asset type: Gotcha! visual development  
Style name: Resonance Atelier  
Primary request: [describe the specific scene, screen, object, or character here]

Create the asset in the Gotcha! Resonance Atelier style: clean stylized 3D adventure-drama, painterly but simplified, expressive realistic characters, warm cinematic lighting, controlled high contrast, hand-painted material feel without noisy texture, mobile-readable silhouettes, and premium animated game concept art.

The world language is ancient resonance interpreted through modern tools: old-world Archive warmth blended with emerald scientific mystery. Use warm amber key light, soft candle or lamp glow, cool emerald rim light or resonance sources, controlled shadows, soft atmospheric haze, and restrained dust motes. The palette should center on deep emerald, aged brass gold, dark walnut, parchment cream, warm amber, dark indigo shadows, muted leather browns, and subtle oxidized green.

Materials should feel grounded and tactile: simplified painted wood grain, brushed brass, glowing emerald glass, aged leather books, parchment maps, worn metal, archive paper, polished resonance coins, stone tablets, and carefully preserved artifacts. Reduce micro-noise and excessive scratches. Prefer broad clean shapes, soft painterly transitions, clear silhouettes, and selective detail only on story-critical objects.

Composition should be cinematic and mobile-first: strong focal hierarchy, a clear foreground artifact or character anchor, uncluttered background storytelling, and safe space for dialogue or UI when needed. The mood is sincere, mysterious, inviting, and premium, with wonder instead of horror and restraint instead of spectacle.

## Character / Tutorial Scene Add-On

Show the character with grounded emotion and believable age, posture, and history. Faces should be expressive but not exaggerated. Clothing should feel classic, layered, and worn with purpose: waistcoats, jackets, field gear, scarves, old watches, glasses, leather, brass details, and muted earth tones. Background objects should tell story through shape and placement: old expedition photos, notebooks, maps, books, fossils, instruments, and the resonance lens. Keep the character human, sincere, and dimensional rather than cartoonish.

## Lens / Cipher Add-On

The resonance lens should feel like an ancient scientific instrument: brass rings, emerald glass, precise compass-like markings, subtle glyph engravings, and green light that behaves like sound made visible. When activated, the glow should pulse softly from the center, brightening nearby surfaces with emerald reflections. Cipher interfaces should look tactile and diegetic: rotating rings, glyph plates, harmonic alignment marks, soft light bands, and satisfying mechanical precision.

## Product Archive Reveal Add-On

The product should appear as an archived resonance match, not an advertisement. Isolate the product on an in-world Archive surface or pedestal with warm light, brass trim, parchment labels, resonance serial lines, and faint emerald glow. Include the product shape clearly enough for recognition, but avoid shelf context, retail signage, prices, coupons, or promotional copy. The barcode or resonance code may be shown as a tasteful etched serial pattern associated with the recovered treasure.

## Map / Anomaly Add-On

Use modern GPS map logic with ancient resonance styling layered over it. Anomalies should appear as elegant emerald-gold signatures, rings, compass bearings, faint harmonic ripples, or lens projections. The map should remain readable and practical, with the Gotcha! visual language expressed through framing, overlays, glows, and icons rather than obscuring navigation.

## Echo / Treasure Reveal Add-On

Treasures should feel like recovered pieces of lost history. Use gold resonance coins, stone fragments, glyphs, emerald light, and visible sound-wave motifs. Echo moments should suggest audio becoming visible: soft harmonic waves, floating dust, subtle vibration, and light passing across brass and parchment. The reveal should feel intimate and uncanny, as if an ancient memory has briefly become audible.

## Screen Composition Rules

- Design for vertical mobile framing first.
- Keep focal elements readable at phone size.
- Reserve simple areas for UI, captions, or dialogue when the image will become a screen background.
- Avoid dense object clutter near buttons, scan windows, map controls, and dialogue areas.
- Use high contrast around interaction points, but keep the overall image warm and atmospheric.
- Favor layered depth: foreground artifact, midground character or interface, background Archive environment.

## Negative Prompt

Avoid: hyperreal photography, flat cartoon style, anime, heavy comic-book cross-hatching, excessive painterly noise, gritty horror, cluttered shelves, readable fake text, real brands or logos, advertising design, price tags, coupons, UI overload, exaggerated caricature, plastic toy look, muddy colors, dull emerald glow, generic fantasy armor, medieval tavern styling, sci-fi neon overload, and direct imitation of any existing film, game, show, or studio style.
